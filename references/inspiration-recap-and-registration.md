# Inspiration log: "everything together" as a recap, registration and windows (film 6, v3)

Why this file exists: the client called the "together" act of film 6 (17 s to the end) weak twice. The first fix (orbiting words, dust, whirl) still read as a stack of effects. This log is the research behind the third version. Method and honesty note:

- **Read, not watched.** The entries below come from published descriptions, breakdown interviews and credits of recent motion-design work (Stash Media and Motionographer, 2025 to 2026), plus a few classic lineages recalled from general knowledge (marked *lineage*). No video was watched; each entry records the *idea* the write-up states and what it changed in the film. Do not quote them as visual proof.
- Every idea is followed by a verdict: **used**, **adapted**, or **rejected (why)**.

## C. What changed in film 6 because of this

1. **Concept**: "the name is made of every world of the film". From 17.9 s the plate registers into colour separation, then a window opens out of the plate and each world (HUM, PING, SOUND, MOTION, FRAME, PLATE, ...) is shrunk into one letter of *motioner.* on a beat. The whole film is recapped inside its own name, then the seed drops in as the full stop.
2. **Removed**: the whirl, dust blow-away and iris collapse at 21 s to 24 s (the "forced" transition). The move from the living name to the wordmark is now a lock (a click, a lens of light), not a dissolve.
3. **Sound**: every hard cut in the back half was found by `find_cuts.py` and traced to its cue. What was left are the intended silences before drops (riser or rewind ending 40 ms before the hit, faded with a cosine); the glitch and noise layers were removed from the montage.
